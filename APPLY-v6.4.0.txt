4. NEW COLLECTIONS ANALYTICS
After deployment, open:
https://ruffneck-social-hub.vercel.app/platform/billing-analytics

The page is restricted to platform billing administrators. It offers 30-, 90- and 365-day reporting windows, review-state counts, approved payment totals grouped separately by currency, collection trends, source counts for webhooks vs manual reconciliation, and a downloadable CSV.
The page and export endpoint are read-only. The CSV is capped at 10,000 newest rows and excludes customer email, workspace IDs, card data and review notes. A cap indicator is returned when more rows exist.

Only records with review_status=approved, provider_status=successful and invoice_match=true are counted as collections. Pending and needs-attention amounts are displayed separately, not counted as money collected. Different currencies are never added together.

5. PAYMENT-SAFETY BOUNDARY
The existing v3 Public-Key/manual-review model remains in force. A webhook, redirect or missing-webhook reconciliation record does not activate a subscription. A platform billing administrator must independently inspect the payment in Flutterwave Dashboard and complete the separate APPROVE process.
Missing-webhook reconciliation is limited to successful initial payments that exactly match a still-pending RuffNeck invoice. Do not use it for recurring renewals, failed/pending charges, refunds, reversals or mismatched invoices.
