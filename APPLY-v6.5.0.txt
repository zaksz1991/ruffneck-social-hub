WHAT v6.5.0 ADDS
- Platform administrator lifecycle console at /platform/billing-lifecycle.
- Workspace owner/admin subscription page at /billing/subscription.
- End-of-period cancellation request and withdrawal.
- Separate provider-cancellation confirmation after an administrator cancels the recurring plan in Flutterwave Dashboard.
- Manual past-due transition after the paid period ends; starts a five-day grace period.
- Administrator-controlled grace extension from 1 to 30 additional days.
- Expiry only after the grace deadline and a fresh provider-dashboard check.
- Scheduled cancellation finalization only after provider cancellation is confirmed and the paid period has ended.
- Append-only lifecycle event history available to platform administrators.
- A confirmed renewal clears stale cancellation and grace-period flags.
- Health classifications for healthy, renewal due, renewal review, cancellation, grace, past due, expiry and cancelled states.
- The v6.4 Flutterwave operations, missing-webhook and collections audits now accept compatible later versions instead of asserting a stale exact package version.

IMPORTANT PROVIDER LIMITATION
This is still the Public-Key-only/manual-review Flutterwave mode. A workspace cancellation request records the request in RuffNeck; it does not call Flutterwave and does not stop provider billing. A platform administrator must cancel the recurring plan in Flutterwave Dashboard, then record the provider cancellation confirmation in RuffNeck. RuffNeck keeps access through the paid period and only finalizes cancellation after the period ends.

There is no cron job or automatic background expiry processor in this release. Platform administrators must review the lifecycle console and inspect Flutterwave before marking past due, granting grace, expiring a subscription or finalizing cancellation. Successful charges and renewals still require the existing manual payment approval flow. No browser redirect or webhook alone activates a subscription.

VERCEL / ENVIRONMENT
- No new environment variables are required.
- Do not change NEXT_PUBLIC_FLW_PUBLIC_KEY or FLW_SECRET_HASH for this release.
- Keep the Supabase service-role key server-side only.
- Do not invent or add a Flutterwave Secret Key. Automated provider cancellation and transaction verification remain unavailable without the correct merchant Secret Key.
